You can activate your BMO debit card using several convenient methods. BMO Bank of Montreal gives you the flexibility to activate your card at a BMO ATM, through BMO online banking, by calling customer service, or in person at a branch. This guide covers every activation method so you can choose the one that works best for you.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Activate at a BMO ATM (Most Common)">
    <p>For most BMO debit cards and access cards, activation happens when you complete your first PIN selection at a BMO ATM. This is the most common and secure method.</p>

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Find a BMO ATM">
        <p>Locate a BMO Bank of Montreal ATM near you using the <a href="https://www.bmo.com/locations">BMO branch locator</a>.</p>
      </Step>
      <Step title="Insert your card">
        <p>Insert your new BMO debit card into the ATM. The machine will recognize that it needs activation.</p>
      </Step>
      <Step title="Set your PIN">
        <p>Follow the prompts to create a secure 4-digit PIN. You must enter it twice to confirm.</p>
      </Step>
      <Step title="Complete a transaction">
        <p>Withdraw cash, check your balance, or complete another transaction to finalize the activation. Your card is now ready for use.</p>
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Activate Online via bmo.com/activate or Online Banking">
    <p>You can also activate your BMO debit card online through the official activation page or your BMO online banking account.</p>

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Visit bmo.com/activate">
        <p>Go to the official BMO card activation URL: <a href="https://www.bmo.com/activate">[www.bmo.com/activate](http://www.bmo.com/activate)</a>. Sign in to BMO online banking.</p>
      </Step>
      <Step title="Select your card">
        <p>Choose the debit card or access card you want to activate from your account dashboard.</p>
      </Step>
      <Step title="Confirm activation">
        <p>Click <strong>Activate Now</strong> and follow the on-screen prompts. Some cards still require you to set your PIN at a BMO ATM afterward.</p>
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Activate by Phone">
    <p>If you prefer phone activation, BMO customer service can assist you.</p>

    <ol>
  <li>Dial the BMO debit card activation phone number: <strong>1-800-363-9992</strong>.</li>
  <li>Follow the automated prompts or speak with a representative.</li>
  <li>Provide your card number and verify your identity.</li>
  <li>Confirm activation when prompted.</li>
</ol>

    <Note>
      Some BMO debit cards still require you to set your PIN at a BMO ATM even after phone activation.
    </Note>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Activate at a BMO Branch">
    <p>If you need help in person, visit any BMO Bank of Montreal branch.</p>

    <ol>
  <li>Bring a valid government-issued photo ID and your new debit card.</li>
  <li>Speak with a bank teller or customer service representative.</li>
  <li>They will activate your card and assist with PIN setup if needed.</li>
</ol>
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Tip>
  If you are activating a replacement BMO debit card, your old card will be deactivated once the new card is successfully activated. Destroy and discard your old card safely.
</Tip>

<Warning>
  You must activate your BMO debit card before you can use it for purchases, ATM withdrawals, or online transactions. Do not share your PIN with anyone.
</Warning>
