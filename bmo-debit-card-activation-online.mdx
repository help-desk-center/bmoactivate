## Activate Your BMO Debit Card Online at bmo.com/activate

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to the activation page">
    <p>Visit the official BMO activation URL: <a href="https://www.bmo.com/activate">[https://www.bmo.com/activate](https://www.bmo.com/activate)</a> in your web browser.</p>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in to BMO online banking">
    <p>Click <strong>Sign In</strong> and enter your BMO online banking username and password. If you do not have online banking, you will need to register first.</p>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select your debit card">
    <p>From your account dashboard, locate the new debit card or access card and click <strong>Activate Now</strong>.</p>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify your card details">
    <p>Review the last four digits of your card number to confirm you are activating the correct card.</p>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete activation">
    <p>Follow the prompts to confirm activation. You may see a success message confirming that your BMO debit card activation online is complete.</p>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Activate Using the BMO Mobile App

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the BMO app">
    <p>Launch the BMO mobile banking app on your iPhone or Android device. Sign in with your credentials.</p>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the activation prompt">
    <p>Look for a notification or banner prompting you to activate your new card. You can also navigate to the card management section.</p>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Activate">
    <p>Select your new debit card and tap <strong>Activate</strong>. Confirm your choice.</p>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Finish setup">
    <p>Once activated, you can manage your card settings, including online purchase permissions, directly in the app.</p>
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  For some BMO debit cards, online activation enables the card but you still need to visit a BMO ATM to set or change your PIN before making ATM withdrawals or debit purchases requiring PIN entry.
</Note>

<Tip>
  If you do not see an activation option in your online banking or app, wait 24 to 48 hours after receiving your card. The card must be fully processed in BMO's system before online activation becomes available.
</Tip>

## Troubleshooting Online Activation

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Card not showing in online banking">
    <p>Ensure your card was mailed to the correct address. If you recently moved, your card may be at your old address. Contact BMO customer service at <strong>1-800-363-9992</strong> for help.</p>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Activation page not loading">
    <p>Clear your browser cache and cookies, then try again. Use a supported browser like Chrome, Safari, Edge, or Firefox.</p>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Forgot online banking password">
    <p>Click <strong>Forgot Password</strong> on the sign-in screen. Follow the prompts to reset your password before activating your card.</p>
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
