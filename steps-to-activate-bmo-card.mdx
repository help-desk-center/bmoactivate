Activating your BMO card is straightforward regardless of whether you have a credit card or debit card. This quick reference covers everything you need to complete the BMO card activation process in under two minutes.

<Card title="Click Here to Activate BMO Card" icon="arrow-right" href="https://www.bmo.com/activate" cta="Activate Now →" arrow="true">
  Visit the official BMO card activation portal at bmo.com/activate
</Card>

## What You Need Before You Start

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Your BMO Card" icon="credit-card">
    Card number, expiry date, and CVV code (3 digits on the back)
  </Card>

  <Card title="Online Banking Access" icon="lock">
    Your BMO online banking username and password, or a phone to call BMO
  </Card>

  <Card title="Personal ID" icon="id-card">
    Last 4 digits of your Social Insurance Number (SIN) may be required
  </Card>

  <Card title="Internet Connection" icon="wifi">
    A stable connection to access bmo.com/activate or the BMO mobile app
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Fastest Method: Activate Online at bmo.com/activate

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to the official activation page">
    Open a browser and navigate to [https://www.bmo.com/activate](https://www.bmo.com/activate).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in to BMO online banking">
    Enter your username and password, then click **Sign In**. New users should click **Register Now** first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your card details">
    Input your card number, expiry date, and CVV when prompted.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Activate Now">
    Press **Activate Now** and wait for the on-screen confirmation. Done.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Your BMO card is active immediately after the confirmation screen appears at bmo.com/activate. No waiting required.
</Tip>

## Phone Activation Steps (If You Prefer)

<Steps>
  <Step title="Call the right number">
    Credit card: **1-877-225-5266**. Debit card: **1-800-363-9992**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select card activation from the menu">
    Follow the automated prompts and choose the card activation option.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your card and personal details">
    Provide your card number, expiry date, and any verification info requested.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Hear the confirmation">
    Wait for the automated confirmation before hanging up.
  </Step>
</Steps>
