You need to activate your BMO bank card before you can use it for purchases, cash withdrawals, or online transactions. BMO offers three main ways to activate: online through [bmo.com/activate](https://www.bmo.com/activate), by phone, or at a BMO ATM. Choose the method that works best for you below.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Activate Online at bmo.com/activate (Recommended)">
    The fastest and most convenient way to activate your BMO card is through the official activation page.

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open the activation page">
        Visit [https://www.bmo.com/activate](https://www.bmo.com/activate) in a web browser.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Sign in to BMO online banking">
        Enter your BMO online banking username and password. If you are not registered, click **Register Now** to set up online banking first, then return to activate.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Enter your card information">
        Provide your new card number, expiry date, and the CVV code on the back.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Confirm activation">
        Click **Activate Now** and wait for the on-screen confirmation. You can use your card immediately.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    <Tip>
      Have your BMO online banking login and card details ready before starting to avoid timing out.
    </Tip>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Activate by Phone">
    If you prefer to activate your card by phone, BMO provides dedicated lines for credit and debit cards.

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Call the correct activation number">
        For BMO credit cards, call **1-877-225-5266**. For BMO debit and banking cards, call **1-800-363-9992**. Both lines are available 24/7.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Follow the automated prompts">
        Select the card activation option from the main menu.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Provide your card details">
        Enter or speak your card number and other identifying information when asked.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Wait for confirmation">
        Listen for the confirmation message before ending the call.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    <Note>
      Keep your card handy during the call. A representative may ask for additional verification if needed.
    </Note>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Activate at a BMO ATM">
    You can also activate your card by making a transaction at any BMO ATM.

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Visit a BMO ATM">
        Find a BMO branch or ATM location near you using the BMO branch locator.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Insert your card">
        Insert the new card into the ATM and enter the temporary PIN provided in your card package.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Complete a transaction">
        Select **Balance Inquiry** or perform a small transaction to trigger activation.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Confirm your card works">
        Keep the receipt and test the card for future purchases.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    <Warning>
      Do not share your PIN with anyone. BMO will never ask for your full PIN over the phone or by email.
    </Warning>
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
