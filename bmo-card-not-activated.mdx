## How to Check If Your BMO Card Is Activated

<Steps>
  <Step title="Try a small purchase">
    Attempt a small transaction at a store or online. A BMO card that is not activated will be declined with a message like "card not activated" or "authorization denied."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check BMO Online Banking">
    Sign in to BMO Online Banking and review your card details. If the card is active, it will show a normal status. A recently replaced or new card may appear with an activation notice.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the BMO mobile app">
    Open the BMO app, go to your account dashboard, and look for any alerts that say your card needs to be activated.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Common Signs That Your BMO Card Is Not Activated

- Your transaction is declined at a store or online merchant
- An ATM says your card is not valid or not authorized
- You receive an SMS or email reminder from BMO to activate your card
- The card does not appear fully active in the BMO mobile app

<Warning>
  Do not use your BMO card at a merchant before activation. Businesses may place a temporary authorization hold that can block funds, even if the transaction itself is declined. Always activate your card as soon as you receive it.
</Warning>

## What Can Delay Activation

Several situations can cause a delay between receiving your card and having it fully active:

- **You activated it but the system has not yet updated.** In rare cases, activation can take a few minutes to an hour to fully propagate across BMO's payment network.
- **You have not received a new PIN.** Some BMO cards require a PIN to be set up before the card is considered fully active.
- **The card was lost in transit and you received a replacement.** If the original card was reissued, the new card may have a different activation timeline.
- **You activated it by phone but have not fully completed prompts.** Automated phone activation requires you to finish all steps, including confirming the last four digits or ZIP/postal code.

## How to Activate Your BMO Card Right Now

Most BMO cards can be activated in one of three ways. Choose the method that works best for you:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Activate online at bmo.com/activate">
    Go to [**https://www.bmo.com/activate**](https://www.bmo.com/activate), enter your full 16-digit card number, expiry date, and CVV, then follow the prompts to complete activation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Activate in the BMO mobile app">
    Open the BMO app, sign in, tap your new card in the dashboard, and select **Activate Card**. Follow the on-screen steps to finish.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Activate by phone">
    Call the BMO card activation number. For credit cards: **1-877-225-5266**. For debit and banking cards: **1-800-363-9992**. Follow the automated prompts.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  If you just activated your BMO card and a purchase is still being declined, wait 5-10 minutes and try again. If the issue persists after an hour, contact BMO customer service.
</Tip>
