## Before You Start

Have these items ready:

- Your new BMO credit card (card number, expiry date, and CVV)
- Your BMO online banking username and password
- Last four digits of your Social Insurance Number (SIN)

## How to Activate Your BMO Credit Card at bmo.com/activate

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the official activation page">
    Go to [https://www.bmo.com/activate](https://www.bmo.com/activate) in a web browser. This is the secure, official BMO card activation portal.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in to BMO online banking">
    Click **Sign In** and enter your BMO online banking username and password. If you are not enrolled, select **Register for Online Banking** first, then return to activate.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select your new card">
    After signing in, look for the prompt to activate a new card. Click **Activate Now** next to the card you received.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm your card details">
    Enter your new card number, expiry date, and CVV when prompted. Verify that the details match your physical card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete activation">
    Review the confirmation screen and click **Confirm**. Your BMO credit card is now active and ready to use.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Most BMO credit card activations are processed within seconds. You can start using your card for purchases immediately after you see the confirmation message on bmo.com/activate.
</Tip>

## Activate by Phone

If you prefer to activate your BMO credit card by phone, call BMO credit card customer service at **1-877-225-5266**. Follow the automated prompts or speak with a representative to complete your BMO credit card activation. This line is available 24/7.

## Troubleshooting

If you do not see your new card in BMO online banking, confirm your mailing address on file is current. If the bmo.com/activate page does not load, clear your browser cache or try a different browser.

<Note>
  If you continue to have trouble activating your BMO credit card online, contact BMO customer service at 1-877-225-5266 for assistance.
</Note>
