If you received a new or replacement BMO debit card, you need to complete BMO card activation before you can make purchases, withdraw cash, or use online banking features. This guide shows you exactly how to activate BMO debit card step by step, whether you prefer to do it online, by phone, or at a BMO ATM. It also covers how to set your PIN, enable online purchases, and safely dispose of your old card.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Receive and inspect your new card">
    <p>Once your new or replacement BMO debit card arrives by mail, check that your name is printed correctly and the envelope has not been tampered with. The card should be attached to a letter with activation instructions.</p>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your activation method">
    <p>You have three ways to activate your BMO new debit card: online at <a href="https://www.bmo.com/activate">[www.bmo.com/activate](http://www.bmo.com/activate)</a>, by calling the BMO card activation number, or at a BMO ATM. Pick the method that is most convenient for you.</p>

    <CardGroup cols={3}>
      <Card title="Online" icon="globe">
        Visit <a href="https://www.bmo.com/activate">bmo.com/activate</a> and sign in to BMO online banking activate card services
      </Card>

      <Card title="By Phone" icon="phone">
        Call <strong>1-800-363-9992</strong> (BMO card activation phone number for banking/debit)
      </Card>

      <Card title="At ATM" icon="credit-card">
        Insert your card at any BMO ATM and follow the prompts to set your PIN
      </Card>
    </CardGroup>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Activate online or by phone">
    <p>If activating online, go to <a href="https://www.bmo.com/activate">bmo.com/activate</a>, sign in, select your new card, and click <strong>Activate Now</strong>. If activating by phone, call <strong>1-800-363-9992</strong>, enter your card number, and follow the automated instructions or speak with a representative to complete BMO activate card.</p>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set or confirm your PIN at a BMO ATM">
    <p>Most BMO debit cards require you to set your PIN at a BMO ATM before the card is fully active. Insert your card, select the PIN setup option, and enter a secure 4-digit PIN twice. Then complete a small transaction (like a balance inquiry) to confirm activation.</p>

    <Note>
      If you already set a PIN through the BMO app or online banking, you may only need to confirm it at the ATM.
    </Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable online and contactless purchases">
    <p>Log in to BMO online banking or open the BMO mobile app. Go to your card settings and enable online purchases, tap payments, and international transactions if needed. This step is optional but recommended so you can use your card everywhere.</p>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Destroy your old card">
    <p>Once you confirm your new BMO debit card activation is successful, cut up your old card through the chip and magnetic stripe. Dispose of the pieces in separate trash bins to protect your account information.</p>
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Your old card will stop working as soon as your new card is activated. Do not attempt to use it after activation.
</Warning>

<Tip>
  If you are a new BMO account holder activating your first debit card, BMO online banking activate card may require you to complete identity verification before you can sign in. Have your account details and identification ready.
</Tip>
