This FAQ covers the most common questions about BMO card activation, including how long activation takes, what you need to get started, and what to do if you run into problems at **bmo.com/activate** or on the BMO mobile app.

<Card title="Click Here to Activate BMO Card" icon="arrow-right" href="https://www.bmo.com/activate" cta="Activate Now" arrow="true">
  Visit the official BMO card activation portal at bmo.com/activate
</Card>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How long does BMO card activation take?">
    BMO card activation is usually instant. When you activate at [**https://www.bmo.com/activate**](https://www.bmo.com/activate), by phone, or through the BMO mobile app, your card should become active within a few minutes. In rare cases, it may take up to one hour for the activation to fully process across BMO's payment network.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I activate my BMO card before it arrives?">
    No. You must have the physical BMO card in hand before you can activate it. Activation requires the 16-digit card number, expiry date, and CVV printed on the card itself. These details are not available before delivery.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What do I need to activate my BMO card online?">
    To activate your BMO card online at **bmo.com/activate**, you need:

    - Your 16-digit BMO card number
    - The card expiry date (month/year)
    - The CVV security code (3 digits on the back for most cards, 4 digits on the front for some credit cards)
    - Your postal code or the last four digits of your phone number for identity verification

    You do not need a BMO Online Banking account to use the **bmo.com/activate** page.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What is the BMO card activation phone number?">
    The BMO card activation phone numbers are:

    - BMO credit cards: **1-877-225-5266**
    - BMO debit and banking cards: **1-800-363-9992**

    Both lines offer a 24/7 automated activation system.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I activate my BMO card on the mobile app?">
    To activate your BMO card on the mobile app:

    1. Open the BMO app and sign in.
    2. Find your new or replacement card on the dashboard.
    3. Tap **Activate Card** and follow the on-screen prompts.
    4. Confirm your details to complete activation.

    If you do not see an activation option, make sure the app is updated to the latest version.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What if bmo.com/activate is not working?">
    If **bmo.com/activate** is not loading or giving errors:

    1. Clear your browser cache and cookies, then try again.
    2. Try a different browser or device.
    3. Use the BMO mobile app to activate your card instead.
    4. Call the BMO card activation phone number as a backup option.

    If the site is down for maintenance, wait one to two hours and try again.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I activate a BMO card without online banking?">
    Yes. You can activate your BMO card without having or using BMO Online Banking. Use the standalone **bmo.com/activate** page with your card details, or call the automated activation phone line:

    - Credit cards: **1-877-225-5266**
    - Debit/banking: **1-800-363-9992**
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I know my BMO card is activated?">
    You can confirm your BMO card is activated by:

    - Attempting a small purchase at a store or online (the transaction should be approved)
    - Checking the BMO mobile app to see if your card shows an active status
    - Reviewing BMO Online Banking for any pending activation alerts

    If your card is still being declined after activation, wait 10 minutes and try again.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What is a BMO activation code?">
    A BMO activation code is sometimes sent by mail or SMS when you receive a replacement card or open a new BMO account. It is used to verify your identity during the activation process. If you are asked for an activation code and do not have one, call BMO customer service at the number on your card.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I activate a replacement BMO card?">
    To activate a replacement BMO card:

    1. Go to [**https://www.bmo.com/activate**](https://www.bmo.com/activate) or open the BMO mobile app.
    2. Enter the new card number, expiry date, and CVV.
    3. Follow the prompts to confirm your identity.
    4. Your old card will be deactivated automatically once the replacement is activated.

    If you have any issues, call the BMO card activation number for your card type.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I use my BMO card immediately after activation?">
    Yes. In most cases, you can use your BMO card for purchases, ATM withdrawals, and online transactions immediately after activation. Allow up to 10 minutes for the activation to fully process before attempting your first transaction.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I contact BMO if activation fails?">
    If BMO card activation fails after trying **bmo.com/activate**, the mobile app, and the phone line, contact BMO customer service:

    - BMO credit cards: **1-877-225-5266**
    - BMO debit and banking: **1-800-363-9992**

    Have your card number, postal code, and a description of the error ready before you call.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Tip>
  Most activation questions are answered fastest by trying **bmo.com/activate** first. If you ever need to speak with someone, BMO's automated activation line is available 24/7.
</Tip>
