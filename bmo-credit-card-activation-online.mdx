BMO credit card activation online can be completed in two ways: through the BMO website at [bmo.com/activate](https://www.bmo.com/activate) or through the BMO Mobile Banking app. Both methods are fast, secure, and available 24/7 without needing to visit a branch or call customer service.

## What You Need Ready

Before you start, gather these details to avoid any delays:

- Your new BMO credit card (card number, expiry date, 3-digit CVV on the back)
- BMO online banking username and password
- Last 4 digits of your Social Insurance Number (SIN)
- A device with an internet connection

## Method 1: Activate via bmo.com/activate (Desktop or Mobile Browser)

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to bmo.com/activate">
    Open a browser and visit [https://www.bmo.com/activate](https://www.bmo.com/activate).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select 'Yes' to confirm online banking">
    When asked if you currently bank online with BMO, select **Yes** and click **Next**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in to BMO online banking">
    Enter your username and password on the secure BMO sign-in page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your credit card details">
    Type in your card number, expiry date, and CVV when prompted.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm and activate">
    Click **Activate Now**. The confirmation screen confirms your BMO credit card is now active.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Method 2: Activate via the BMO Mobile App

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the BMO Mobile Banking app">
    Launch the BMO app on your iPhone or Android phone. Download it from the App Store or Google Play if needed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Enter your BMO online banking credentials and tap **Sign In**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find your new card">
    Tap **My Cards** or navigate to your credit card account. Your new card should appear as pending activation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Activate Card">
    Select **Activate Card** and follow the on-screen prompts to enter your card details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm activation">
    Tap **Confirm**. Your BMO credit card is now activated and ready for use.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Both methods activate your card instantly. If you do not see your new card in the BMO app, sign out and sign back in to refresh your account.
</Note>

<Warning>
  Always use a secure, private internet connection when accessing bmo.com/activate or the BMO app. Avoid public Wi-Fi networks when entering card or banking details.
</Warning>
