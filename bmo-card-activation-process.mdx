Activating your BMO card is a required security step that links your physical card to your BMO account and authorizes it for transactions. Without activation, your card will be declined at every point of sale, ATM, and online checkout. The process takes under two minutes and can be done online at bmo.com/activate, by phone, or at an ATM.

<Card title="Click Here to Activate BMO Card" icon="arrow-right" href="https://www.bmo.com/activate" cta="Activate Now →" arrow="true">
  Visit the official BMO card activation portal at bmo.com/activate
</Card>

## Why Does BMO Require Card Activation?

BMO requires card activation to protect you in case your card is lost or intercepted during mail delivery. An unactivated card cannot be used for purchases. Activation confirms that the card has been received by the right person and is ready to be used securely.

## What You Need to Activate

Before starting the BMO card activation process, have these on hand:

- Your new BMO credit card or debit card
- Your card number, expiry date, and CVV (found on the card)
- Your BMO online banking username and password (for online activation)
- The last four digits of your Social Insurance Number (SIN)

## The BMO Card Activation Process Step by Step

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose your activation method">
    Decide how you want to activate: online at bmo.com/activate, via the BMO mobile app, by phone, or at a BMO ATM. Online activation at [https://www.bmo.com/activate](https://www.bmo.com/activate) is the fastest option.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in or call BMO">
    For online activation, sign in to BMO online banking. For phone activation, call 1-877-225-5266 (credit cards) or 1-800-363-9992 (debit cards).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Provide your card details">
    Enter your card number, expiry date, and CVV when prompted by the website, app, or automated phone system.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm and receive confirmation">
    Click **Activate Now** online or confirm verbally by phone. BMO processes the activation immediately and displays or announces a confirmation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start using your card">
    Your card is active as soon as you see or hear the confirmation. You can use it for purchases, ATM withdrawals, and online payments right away.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How Long Does BMO Card Activation Take?

<Note>
  BMO card activation is processed instantly. Once you complete the steps at bmo.com/activate or on the phone, your card is live within seconds. There is no waiting period.
</Note>

## What to Do After Activation

After completing your BMO card activation, consider these next steps:

- **Set your PIN**: If you have not already set a PIN, visit a BMO ATM to select a 4-digit PIN for debit purchases and cash withdrawals.
- **Add to digital wallet**: Register your card in Apple Pay, Google Pay, or Samsung Pay for contactless payments.
- **Set up account alerts**: Turn on transaction notifications in BMO online banking or the BMO app to monitor card activity.
- **Destroy your old card**: If this is a replacement card, cut up and dispose of the old one.
