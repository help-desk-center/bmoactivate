To activate your BMO card, visit [bmo.com/activate](https://www.bmo.com/activate) and sign in to BMO online banking, or call BMO at 1-877-225-5266 (credit cards) or 1-800-363-9992 (debit cards). You can also activate using the BMO mobile app. All three methods are free and available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

<Card title="Click Here to Activate BMO Card" icon="arrow-right" href="https://www.bmo.com/activate" cta="Activate Now →" arrow="true">
  Visit the official BMO card activation portal at bmo.com/activate
</Card>

## Method 1: Activate Online at bmo.com/activate

This is the fastest and most recommended way to complete BMO card activation.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Visit bmo.com/activate">
    Open your browser and go to [https://www.bmo.com/activate](https://www.bmo.com/activate). This is the official BMO card activation page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in to BMO online banking">
    Enter your BMO online banking username and password and click **Sign In**. If you have not registered for online banking yet, click **Register Now** and complete the setup before returning to activate.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your new card details">
    Provide your card number, expiry date, and CVV (the 3-digit security code on the back of your card).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Activate Now">
    Select **Activate Now** and wait for the confirmation message. Your card is active immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Method 2: Activate by Phone

Call BMO's dedicated activation lines to complete BMO card activation over the phone.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Call the correct BMO number">
    For BMO credit card activation, call **1-877-225-5266**. For BMO debit card activation, call **1-800-363-9992**. Both lines are available 24/7.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose card activation from the menu">
    Follow the automated prompts and select the option for card activation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your card and personal details">
    When prompted, enter your card number, expiry date, and your date of birth or last four digits of your SIN.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm activation">
    Wait for the automated system to confirm that your BMO card activation is complete.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Have your card ready before calling. The automated system requires you to enter the card number and verification details to proceed.
</Note>

## Method 3: Activate via the BMO Mobile App

<Steps>
  <Step title="Download the BMO app">
    Install the BMO Mobile Banking app from the Apple App Store or Google Play Store if you have not already.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Open the app and sign in with your BMO online banking credentials.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to My Cards">
    From the main menu, tap **My Cards** or navigate to your card account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Activate">
    Find your new card listed and tap **Activate Card**. Follow the on-screen steps to complete BMO card activation.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  The BMO app is the most convenient option if you are away from a computer. It works for both BMO credit card activation and BMO debit card activation.
</Tip>
