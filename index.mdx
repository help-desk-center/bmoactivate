## Quickest Way: Activate Online at bmo.com/activate

The fastest way to activate your BMO card is through the official activation portal. Follow these steps:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to bmo.com/activate">
    Open your browser and visit [https://www.bmo.com/activate](https://www.bmo.com/activate).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in or register">
    Enter your BMO online banking login credentials. If you do not have an account, select **Register Now** to create one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your card details">
    Input your card number, expiration date, and CVV when prompted.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Activate">
    Click **Activate Now** and wait for the confirmation message. Your card is active immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  The direct link to activate your card online is [https://www.bmo.com/activate](https://www.bmo.com/activate). Have your card and online banking details ready before you start.
</Note>
