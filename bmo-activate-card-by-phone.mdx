## BMO Credit Card Activation by Phone

For BMO credit cards, call the dedicated activation line.

<CardGroup cols={1}>
  <Card title="BMO Credit Card Activation Number" icon="phone">
    **1-877-225-5266**

    Available 24/7. Automated system available in English and French.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Dial the Activation Number">
    Call 1-877-225-5266 from the phone number associated with your BMO account, if possible.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter Your Card Number">
    The IVR (interactive voice response) system will ask for your 16-digit BMO credit card number. Enter it using your keypad.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify Your Identity">
    You may be asked for your date of birth and the last digits of your Social Insurance Number (SIN) to confirm your identity.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Follow the Prompts">
    Listen to the instructions and confirm activation. The system will confirm when your card is active.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## BMO Debit Card Activation by Phone

For BMO debit cards and banking cards, use the banking customer service line.

<CardGroup cols={1}>
  <Card title="BMO Debit Card Activation Number" icon="phone">
    **1-800-363-9992**

    Available 24/7. Connect to the automated system or a live representative.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Dial the Banking Number">
    Call 1-800-363-9992. If calling from outside Canada, use the international number found on the back of your card or on bmo.com.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Card Activation">
    Choose the card activation option from the main menu. If you are not sure, say "activate card" or press 0 for a representative.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Provide Your Information">
    Enter your debit card number and verify your identity with your date of birth and postal code.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm Activation">
    Follow the remaining prompts. The system will confirm once your BMO debit card is active.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What to Have Ready Before You Call

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Credit Cards">
    - Your 16-digit BMO credit card
    - Date of birth (on file with BMO)
    - Last digits of your SIN
  </Card>

  <Card title="Debit Cards">
    - Your BMO debit card
    - Date of birth
    - Postal code associated with your account
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Note>
  Calling during weekday mornings (8 AM to 11 AM ET) often results in shorter wait times if you need to speak with a live agent. The automated IVR system is available instantly at any hour.
</Note>

<Tip>
  Save the BMO card activation number in your phone contacts. Label it "BMO Activate Card" so you can find it quickly in the future.
</Tip>

<Warning>
  BMO will never ask for your full SIN or full password over the phone. If a caller requests this information, hang up and call the official BMO card activation number directly.
</Warning>
