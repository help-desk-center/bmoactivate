<Steps>
  <Step title="Visit bmo.com/activate">
    Open your browser and go to [https://www.bmo.com/activate](https://www.bmo.com/activate). This page is the official BMO card activation portal.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign In to BMO Online Banking">
    Click **Sign In** and enter your BMO online banking username and password. If you have two-factor authentication enabled, complete that step.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to Card Management">
    From the account dashboard, navigate to **My Accounts** or **Card Services**. Find the card that needs activation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Activate the Card">
    Click the card, then click **Activate**. Review the terms, confirm, and your card is ready for use immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Not Registered for BMO Online Banking Yet?

If you do not have a BMO online banking account, you will need to register before you can activate your card.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start Registration">
    On the bmo.com sign-in page, click **Register for Online Banking**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter Your Information">
    Provide your BMO card number, date of birth, and the last digits of your Social Insurance Number (SIN). This verifies your identity.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create Your Login Credentials">
    Choose a username, password, and security questions. Make sure your password is strong and unique to BMO.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm and Sign In">
    Review your details, submit the registration, and sign in. Now you can complete BMO credit card activation online or BMO debit card activation online.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Bookmark [https://www.bmo.com/activate](https://www.bmo.com/activate) for quick access any time you receive a new card.
</Tip>

<Warning>
  Always look for the padlock icon and "https://" in your browser address bar before entering login information. This confirms you are on the real BMO site and not a phishing page.
</Warning>
