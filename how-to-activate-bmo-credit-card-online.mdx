## What You Need

Before visiting bmo.com/activate, make sure you have:

- Your new BMO credit card (card number, expiry date, CVV)
- Your BMO online banking username and password
- Last four digits of your SIN (may be required for verification)
- A supported browser: Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge

## How to Activate Your BMO Credit Card Online

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open bmo.com/activate">
    Go to [https://www.bmo.com/activate](https://www.bmo.com/activate) in your web browser. Look for the "Let's activate your credit card" heading.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm you bank online with BMO">
    Select **Yes** when the page asks if you currently bank online with BMO. If you select **No**, you will be directed to register for BMO online banking first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in to BMO online banking">
    Click **Next** and sign in with your BMO online banking credentials on the secure login page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your card details">
    Provide your credit card number, expiry date, and CVV code when prompted.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete activation">
    Click **Activate Now** and wait for the success confirmation. Your BMO credit card is now active.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  BMO credit card activation online is processed instantly. As soon as you see the confirmation screen, you can use your card for purchases, online payments, and cash advances.
</Tip>

## Frequently Asked Questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do I need online banking to activate my BMO credit card online?">
    Yes. To activate at bmo.com/activate you need an active BMO online banking account. If you do not have one, click **Register Now** on the activation page. After registering, return to bmo.com/activate to complete activation.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What if I forgot my BMO online banking password?">
    Click **Forgot Password** on the BMO sign-in page and follow the steps to reset it. Once your password is reset, return to bmo.com/activate to activate your card.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I activate my BMO credit card before it arrives?">
    No. BMO credit card activation requires the physical card number and CVV, which are printed on the card itself. You cannot complete activation without the card in hand.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What if bmo.com/activate is not working?">
    Try a different browser or clear your cache. If the site is still unavailable, call BMO credit card customer service at 1-877-225-5266 to activate by phone instead.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
